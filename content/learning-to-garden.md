## My issue with Obsidian and other tools

Working within Obsidian or another note-taking tool has the drawback (in my opinion) of making one feel as though you are "learning" from the notes you're taking. In practice, it often feels like the learning involved in "optimizing" these tools for my particular use case eclipses the learning that I actually want to be doing (from my notes). Now, don't get me wrong, I do think these tools are great, low-entry-cost options for non-developers who want a more feature-rich note-taking system, that's fine. However, I feel that it's easy to [mistake the map for the territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Map%E2%80%93territory_relation) in a way, to feel as though your optimizations of your Obsidian folders or tagging system is really allowing you to learn something when the reality is different. The tool for capturing and measuring our notes and their connection is not, itself, learned knowledge.
